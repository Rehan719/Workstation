# Guide to Pleasing Allah (25-Step Framework)

## Phase 1: Internal Foundations
1. **Sincerity (Ikhlas):** Purifying the heart from seeking other than Allah.
2. **Repentance (Tawbah):** Returning to Allah after every slip.
3. **Gratitude (Shukr):** Recognizing blessings and using them to serve the Bestower.
4. **Patience (Sabr):** Steadfastness in trials and in obedience.
5. **Trust (Tawakkul):** Reliance on Allah while taking necessary means.

## Phase 2: Core Acts of Worship
6. **Prayer (Salah):** Establishing a private conversation with the Lord.
7. **Quran:** Recitation, reflection, and daily application.
8. **Dhikr:** Constant remembrance of Allah to keep the heart alive.
9. **Fasting:** Shielding the soul through self-discipline.
10. **Charity (Sadaqah):** Giving what you love for the sake of the Beloved.

## Phase 3: Character & Ethics
11. **Truthfulness:** In speech, action, and intention.
12. **Trustworthiness (Amanah):** Fulfilling duties to Allah and people.
13. **Humility:** Removing pride and treating others with mercy.
14. **Guarding the Tongue:** Avoiding backbiting and useless speech.
15. **Haya (Modesty):** A natural bashfulness before Allah and His creation.

## Phase 4: Social Spiritual Excellence
16. **Upholding Kinship:** Excellence toward parents and family.
17. **Serving Others:** Finding Allah's love in the service of humanity.
18. **Righteous Companionship:** Surrounding oneself with those who remind you of Him.
19. **Commanding Good, Forbidding Evil:** With wisdom and kindness.
20. **Seeking Knowledge:** A lifelong journey to understand the Creator.

## Phase 5: Consistency & Finality
21. **Tahajjud:** Seeking intimacy in the stillness of the night.
22. **Istiqamah:** Consistency in small, regular deeds.
23. **Self-Assessment (Muhasabah):** Taking account of oneself daily.
24. **Dua:** The weapon of the believer and the essence of worship.
25. **Legacy:** Intending for all actions to benefit others after death.
