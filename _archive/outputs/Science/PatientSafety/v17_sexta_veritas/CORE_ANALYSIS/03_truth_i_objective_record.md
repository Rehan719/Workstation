## 1. EMPIRICAL BASELINE
The production intelligence for v17.0 is anchored by three critical datasets that prove the existence of unaddressed safety risks in advanced genetic medicine.

### A. AAV Germline Exposure (Wu et al. 2025)
- **Finding**: Widespread transduction of murine testicular germ cells following AAV2/9 administration.
- **Metric**: >5% transduction efficiency in gonadal tissue.
- **Sovereign Impact**: Challenges the "non-germline" safety narrative currently used in EMA/FDA filings.

### B. Proteomic Instability (Chazarin et al. 2026)
- **Finding**: Chronic activation of C3/C5 complement pathways and persistent autoantibody increases 6 months post-mRNA treatment.
- **Metric**: 214 of 342 tracked proteins significantly altered post-16 weeks.
- **Sovereign Impact**: Mandates Truth III update to long-term post-marketing surveillance.

### C. Capsid Gene Transfer (Gifford et al. 2025)
- **Finding**: Evidence of extensive horizontal gene transfer of AAV capsid sequences across lineages.
- **Metric**: Confirmed transfer in laboratory and feral populations.
- **Sovereign Impact**: Raises new Truth V ethical concerns regarding environmental biocontainment.
