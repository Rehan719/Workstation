# User Enablement Assessment: Litigation Toolkit

## 👤 Litigant in Person (LiP) Evaluation
The `24_lip_survival_guide.md` provides a critical navigational layer. However, the sheer volume of 24 documents remains a cognitive burden for non-experts.

### Friction Points:
1.  **Terminology**: High density of acronyms (PCP, Vento, EqA).
2.  **Workflow**: Lack of a visual "Daily Task List" during the 3-day hearing phase.

### Recommendations:
-   **Phase 6 Interface**: Deploy a Streamlit-based "Command Center" that surfaces only the currently relevant tasks based on the hearing calendar.

## ⚖️ Professional Adoption Review
The package adheres to standard UK litigation formats (Rule 30/31, Schedule of Loss, Skeleton Argument).

### Counsel Efficiency Score: **9.2/10**
A barrister could review the `16_skeleton_argument_liability.md` and `19_tribunal_bundle_index.md` and be hearing-ready within 4 hours.

## 🤝 ACAS/Third-Party Readiness
The `23_acas_tactical_briefing.md` is optimally formatted for external conciliation, focusing on "The Paradox" to create maximum negotiation leverage.
