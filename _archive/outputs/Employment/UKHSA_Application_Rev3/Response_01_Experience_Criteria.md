# Response 01: Experience Criteria (REV3 - Targeted)

This response addresses the "Experience Criteria" identified in the UKHSA Healthcare Scientist (SEO) person specification, demonstrating a "Science-First, Tech-Enhanced" narrative and bridging public health with industrial excellence.

---

## 1. Substantial Recent Experience in Accredited Diagnostics

**Situation:** My career is defined by over 15 years of high-stakes laboratory science, primarily at the National Institute for Biological Standards and Control (NIBSC/MHRA), but also spanning UKHSA (formerly HPA) and Lonza Biologics. At NIBSC, I operated at the global forefront of virological standardisation for nearly a decade.

**Task:** My objective has always been to ensure the accuracy and reliability of infectious disease diagnostics on a national and international scale, particularly for high-priority pathogens like West Nile Virus (WNV), Herpes Simplex Virus (HSV), HIV-2, and Hepatitis A.

**Action:** I managed end-to-end production cycles for CE-marked reagents and WHO International Standards in Containment Level 3 (CL3) environments, ensuring absolute adherence to ISO 13485, GMP, and IVDR guidelines. I led international collaborative studies involving 32 institutions across 24 countries, harmonizing diverse laboratory data into a single, statistically robust benchmark. My recent tenure at Lonza Biologics reinforced this expertise with modern industrial GMP rigour, where I operated AKTA chromatography systems and maintained ALCOA+ compliant batch records.

**Result:** I successfully delivered multiple WHO primary NAT standards that now serve as the global accuracy benchmark. I co-authored four definitive WHO Technical Reports (2017–2020), demonstrating my ability to synthesize complex data for regulatory authorities.

## 2. Leading Complex Projects and Change Management

**Situation:** A critical example of my project leadership was the successful refurbishment of SAPO4/Schedule 5-compliant CL3 laboratories at NIBSC.

**Task:** The mission was to upgrade our high-containment infrastructure to meet new biosecurity standards while maintaining critical WHO production timelines without disruption.

**Action:** I spearheaded the project, negotiating between facilities management, health and safety officers, and senior scientific leads. I managed the transition from legacy systems, orchestrated the IQ/OQ/PQ qualification of new molecular platforms, and implemented updated SOPs. I acted as a bridge between technical engineering requirements and scientific operational needs.

**Result:** The facility was delivered on schedule and achieved 100% compliance with SAPO4 and Schedule 5 standards upon inspection. This ensured NIBSC's continued capability to respond to global health threats without any downtime in reagent production.

## 3. Commitment to Quality Service and Accreditation

**Situation:** As the Senior Laboratory Scientist at the Anthony Nolan Research Institute, I was tasked with leading the technical preparation for a high-stakes UKAS audit to maintain ISO 15189 accreditation.

**Task:** My goal was to ensure that every diagnostic workflow met the highest standards of accuracy, traceability, and patient safety.

**Action:** I performed a granular gap analysis of all LIMS workflows and standardized molecular diagnostic procedures, including ABO blood-group testing on the Immunocor Echo platform. I conducted comprehensive internal audits, implemented corrective actions, and mentored junior staff on ALCOA+ data integrity principles and clean-room etiquette.

**Result:** The laboratory achieved zero major findings in the 2024 UKAS audit. Furthermore, my process optimizations resulted in a 66% reduction in laboratory error rates, demonstrating the direct impact of proactive quality management on service excellence.
