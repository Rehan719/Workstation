# Conversational Spiritual Guidance

This document captures themes from collaborative spiritual reflections.

## Core Themes
- **Love of Allah:** The driving force of all worship.
- **Service to Creation:** As a means of reaching the Creator.
- **Transformative Faith:** Faith that leads to character improvement (Akhlaq).

## Practical Steps
- Establish morning and evening dhikr.
- Cultivate presence in prayer.
- Practice daily acts of anonymous charity.
