# v152.0 Collective Intelligence Audit & Civilization Roadmap

## 1. Executive Summary
v152.0 ("Collective Intelligence") evolves the Workstation from a participatory economy into a self-aware civilization. This document audits existing v151.0 data streams and outlines the integration of real-time intelligence, autonomous governance, and intelligent treasury management.

## 2. Infrastructure Audit & Civilization Brain

### 2.1 Real-Time Data Ingestion
- **Source**: Stripe payments, Polygon transactions, DAO votes, Creator Studio activity.
- **v152.0 Requirement**: Unified event bus to pipe all actions into the "Civilization Brain" SQLite/JSON store for pattern analysis.

### 2.2 Autonomous Governance (AI Delegation)
- **Current**: 100% human-voted proposals.
- **v152.0 Strategy**:
  - **AI Delegation**: DAO-approved categories for AI execution (e.g., small grants, standard fee adjustments).
  - **AI Executor**: Background service implementing DAO rules with human override.

### 2.3 Intelligent Treasury
- **Current**: Human-evaluated grant proposals.
- **v152.0 Strategy**:
  - **Project Evaluation Engine**: Algorithmically score proposals based on reputation, market signals, and risk.
  - **Automated Funding**: Direct allocation to projects exceeding performance thresholds.

### 2.4 Prediction Markets & Reputation 2.0
- **Current**: Static reputation and sentiment.
- **v152.0 Strategy**:
  - **Prediction Markets**: WST-backed betting on civilizational outcomes to harness crowd wisdom.
  - **Dynamic Reputation**: Weighted scores influencing voting power (quadratic + reputation multiplier).

## 3. Implementation Targets
- **Backend**: FastAPI v320 routers for `intelligence`, `governance_autonomous`, and `markets`.
- **Frontend**: Real-time `CivilizationDashboard.tsx` and `PredictionMarket.tsx`.
- **AI**: Local pattern detection models for trend forecasting.
