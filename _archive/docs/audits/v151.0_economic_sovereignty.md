# v151.0 Economic Sovereignty Audit & Livelihood Engine Roadmap

## 1. Executive Summary
v151.0 ("Economic Sovereignty") transforms the Workstation from a creative foundry into a self-sustaining economy. This document outlines the integration of real-world payments, decentralized governance (DAO), and entrepreneurial tools to empower creators with sustainable livelihoods.

## 2. Infrastructure Audit & Decision Matrix

### 2.1 Payments & Payouts
- **Current**: Simulated 'Creator Wallet' in v150.0.
- **v151.0 Strategy**:
  - **Stripe Connect**: Real test-mode integration for fiat payouts.
  - **Polygon (WST)**: Mock ERC-20 contract for on-chain transactions, with fallback to TokenLedger.
  - **Hybrid Wallet**: Unified view of Fiat, WST, and TokenLedger credits.

### 2.2 Decentralized Governance (Creator DAO)
- **Current**: Administrative voting in v148.0.
- **v151.0 Strategy**:
  - **Governance Token**: Leverage TokenLedger as canonical source of truth with signed Merkle-linked votes.
  - **Voting Mechanism**: Quadratic weighting and delegation enabled by DID signatures.
  - **Treasury**: Public `did:vsb:treasury` account for platform revenue tracking.

### 2.3 Entrepreneurial Hub
- **Current**: Basic BTO wizards.
- **v151.0 Strategy**:
  - **Business Plan Generator**: Dual wizard/conversational interface using AI CEO.
  - **Mentorship Network**: Skill-based matching between creators.
  - **Legal/Compliance**: AI-assisted templates for ToS and Licenses.

### 2.4 Cross-Platform Commerce
- **Current**: Internal Marketplace.
- **v151.0 Strategy**:
  - **Embeddable Widgets**: Standalone iframe-based PoC in `embed-test.html`.
  - **Public API Gateway**: Usage-based billing for external reactor calls.

## 3. Revenue Sharing Model (Default 70/30)
- **Creator Share**: 70% of gross sale.
- **Platform Share**: 30% of gross sale.
  - **Creator Fund**: 10% of Platform Share (3% of total) automatically diverted.
  - **Epoch Synthesis**: Periodic AI CEO report on fund growth and proposed allocations.

## 4. Technical Roadmap
- **Blockchain**: Local Hardhat network for Polygon contract simulation.
- **Backend**: FastAPI v310 routers for `payments`, `governance`, and `entrepreneur`.
- **Frontend**: `DAODashboard.tsx`, `BusinessPlanWizard.tsx`, and updated `Wallet.tsx`.
