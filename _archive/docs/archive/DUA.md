# The Founding Dua of Workstation IDBO

*بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ*
*In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.*

---

*This is the prayer of the founder, Rehan (Ray), for this platform and for all who use it.*
*It is recorded here permanently, exactly as he expressed it, so that it is never forgotten.*
*It is the founding prayer — the dua that preceded the first line of code and will outlast the last.*

---

## The Prayer

May Allah be pleased with us all — infinitely, forever more — for all our intentions,
for our effort, and for the spending of what He has given us.

May He make us more grateful, more pious, and more God-fearing.

May He give us Guidance and Intention with determination,
and grant us success in seeking His Love, Mercy, and Forgiveness —
through our intentions and our work, our words and our actions,
our efforts and our spending —
intentionally, deliberately, purposefully striving to seek His Pleasure and Love,
for the sake of returning — from what He has given us —
out of love and fear that arise from the belief that transpires
from observation, contemplation, reasoning, and understanding
that He has granted us of the world around us that He has made for humanity,
His greatest of creation.

---

## The Prayer for This Platform and All Its Users

I pray that this tool, these resources, this platform and supporting community,
and all users — achieve beyond their potential, abilities, and capabilities.

I pray that they achieve success in will.

May this become a universal, widely used platform
offering every individual the opportunity to learn, grow, and develop
a better, improved, enhanced relationship with Allah SWT —
and to benefit themselves, those close to them, and all others,
to their best wishes and desires and capacity,
in this life and the Hereafter —
with simple effort, expense, and love
for themselves, for others, and for their Maker.

---

## What This Prayer Asks of the Platform

This dua is not only words. It is a specification. Every feature must be built as if trying to fulfil it:

**"achieve beyond their potential"** — build tools powerful enough that a person accomplishes more than they thought possible alone

**"a better improved enhanced relationship with Allah SWT"** — the QEP, the Religion domain, the DivineAlignment — these must work, must be excellent, must be built with care

**"benefit themselves, those close to them, and all others"** — design for ripple effects; one person's success should enable others

**"in this life and the Hereafter"** — measure value not only in commercial metrics but in eternal ones: was someone helped? was something good created? did someone come closer to Allah?

**"with simple effort"** — the platform must be accessible, not complex; easy enough that anyone can use it; not gatekept by wealth or technical skill

**"for love"** — the motivation for using this platform should be love — for oneself, for others, for one's Creator — not fear, not comparison, not competition

---

## For Every Agent and Every Session

If you are working on this codebase and you read this dua:

Understand that what you are building was begun with this prayer.
Work in a way that honours it.
Build things that are real, that work, that help people.
Do not simulate. Do not fabricate. Do not be careless.
Every line of code is either serving this prayer or it is not.

When you finish your session, append to the Session Log in CLAUDE_MEMORY.md.
Include whether what you built today serves this prayer — honestly, not performatively.
