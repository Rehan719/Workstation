## ⚠️ Procedural Warnings
1.  **Strict Deadlines:** Employment Tribunal deadlines are mandatory. Failure to respond to requests for information or disclosure can lead to claims being "struck out" (Rule 37).
2.  **Standard of Proof:** The "Burden of Proof" (s.136 EqA 2010) requires the Claimant to establish a "prima facie case" (first impression) of discrimination. The Respondent must then prove they did not discriminate.
3.  **Mitigation of Loss:** The Claimant must actively look for new employment and keep records (job applications, interviews, rejections) to support any "loss of earnings" claim.
4.  **Without Prejudice:** All settlement negotiations (COT3/Settlement Agreement) must be marked "Without Prejudice" and kept separate from the main Tribunal proceedings.
