# Level 5 Lesson 1: An-Nahl

## 1. Introduction to An-Nahl
An-Nahl is a significant chapter of the Quran. This lesson will focus on its meaning and recitation.

## 2. Quranic Concept: Asma al-Husna
**Description:** The Most Beautiful Names of Allah
**Significance:** Understanding Allah's attributes
**Related Verses:** 7:180, 59:24

## 3. Tajweed Rule: Izh-har
**Definition:** Clear pronunciation of Noon Sakinah/Tanween when followed by Throat letters
**Applicable Letters:** ء, ه, ع, ح, غ, خ

## 4. Recitation Practice
Recite the first 5 verses of An-Nahl focusing on the rule of Izh-har.

## 5. Assessment
Complete the quiz at the end of this module to verify your understanding.
