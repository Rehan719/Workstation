# Level 1 Lesson 1: Al-Fatihah

## 1. Introduction to Al-Fatihah
Al-Fatihah is a significant chapter of the Quran. This lesson will focus on its meaning and recitation.

## 2. Quranic Concept: Tawhid
**Description:** Oneness of Allah (SWT)
**Significance:** Foundation of Islamic faith
**Related Verses:** 112:1, 2:255

## 3. Tajweed Rule: Izh-har
**Definition:** Clear pronunciation of Noon Sakinah/Tanween when followed by Throat letters
**Applicable Letters:** ء, ه, ع, ح, غ, خ

## 4. Recitation Practice
Recite the first 5 verses of Al-Fatihah focusing on the rule of Izh-har.

## 5. Assessment
Complete the quiz at the end of this module to verify your understanding.
