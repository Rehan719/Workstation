# Business Model Summary: Long-Term Safety Assurance (LTSA) Suite

## Executive Summary
The LTSA Suite is a production-grade safety monitoring platform for advanced therapies (AAV, CAR-T, mRNA). It addresses the critical "Regulatory Lag" by providing autonomous, real-time patient monitoring.

## Market Opportunity
* **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** $4.2B by 2030.
* **Primary Customers:** Tier-1 Biopharma and Clinical Research Organizations (CROs).
* **Value Proposition:** 30% reduction in clinical trial attrition and 20% faster regulatory approval.

## Service Offering
1. **PGS (Pre-emptive Genomic Screening):** Risk stratification for trial enrollment.
2. **RCM (Real-time Cytokine Monitoring):** AI-driven early warning for neurotoxicity.
3. **SLF (Standardized Long-term Follow-up):** 15-year digital health record persistence.
