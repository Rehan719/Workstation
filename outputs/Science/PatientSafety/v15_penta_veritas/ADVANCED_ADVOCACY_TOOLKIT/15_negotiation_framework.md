# 15: Negotiation Framework — Patient Safety Advocacy

## 1. QUADRA-VERITAS NEGOTIATION MODEL
The v15.0 framework utilizes a non-adversarial, evidence-convergent approach to negotiation.

### A. Establish the Common Ground (Truth I)
- Agree on the baseline scientific reality (e.g., Wu 2025).
- Use SHA-3-512 hashes to ensure "Truth I Inevitability."

### B. Map the Narrative (Truth II)
- Acknowledge stakeholder experiences (e.g., patient anxiety).
- Integrate subjective feedback into the solution design.

### C. Solve for the Gap (Truth III)
- Present the procedural reform as a shared solution to the identified gap.
- Avoid "blame-casting"; focus on "systemic evolution."

### D. Secure the Future (Truth IV)
- Use the predictive model to show the mutual benefit of early adoption.
- Position the agreement as a "Strategic Sovereign" asset.

## 2. TACTICAL RESPONSES
- **If the opponent ignores Truth I**: Pivot to Truth II (Moral/Ethical pressure).
- **If the opponent ignores Truth IV**: Pivot to Truth III (Compliance/Legal pressure).
