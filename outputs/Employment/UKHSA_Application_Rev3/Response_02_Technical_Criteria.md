# Response 02: Technical Criteria (REV3 - Targeted)

This response addresses the "Technical Criteria" identified in the UKHSA Healthcare Scientist (SEO) person specification, highlighting mastery of molecular methods, instrumentation, and the "Interdisciplinary Bridge" of digital automation.

---

## 1. Proficiency in Molecular and Serological Methods

**Situation:** Across my 15-year career at NIBSC/MHRA, UKHSA, and Lonza Biologics, I have developed expert-level proficiency in the full diagnostic lifecycle, from initial assay development to routine high-volume testing and troubleshooting.

**Task:** My focus has been on architecting highly sensitive characterisation, quantitation, and stability assays for diverse pathogens using state-of-the-art molecular and serological platforms.

**Action:** I have mastered a vast array of techniques, including PCR, RT-qPCR, and ddPCR, alongside serological methods like ELISA and IFA. At NIBSC, I led the evaluation of high-throughput molecular systems and successfully introduced droplet digital PCR (ddPCR) for vaccine stability testing. This involved designing the validation matrix and executing comparative sensitivity studies that resulted in a 2-fold increase in detection sensitivity. I have designed and executed precision, accuracy, and limit-of-detection (LOD) studies in alignment with MHRA Annex 6 and IVDR guidelines.

**Result:** My technical expertise ensured the successful validation and production of CE-marked reagents that underpin global diagnostic accuracy. My work at Lonza further expanded this into industrial bioprocessing, operating AKTA chromatography systems and managing downstream workflows like UF/DF and VRF.

## 2. Mastery of Diagnostic Platforms and Instrumentation

**Situation:** Operating in accredited environments requires not just technical skill, but a deep understanding of the instrumentation and the data it generates.

**Task:** To provide reliable diagnostic outputs, I have maintained expert-level operation and troubleshooting capability on premier molecular platforms.

**Action:** My platform mastery includes the Roche Cobas 6800, Diasorin Liaison XL, ABI 7500, and Qiagen Rotagene systems. At Anthony Nolan, I optimized blood-group testing on the Immunocor Echo platform. In each role, I have taken a leading role in assessing new technologies to enhance laboratory capabilities, ensuring that high scientific standards and rigorous validation protocols (IQ/OQ/PQ) are followed.

**Result:** Consistent delivery of high-fidelity data under strict timescales. My ability to rapidly master and evaluate new instrumentation has made me a key technical resource for RACU and PHM operations.

**Situation:** The transition to modern diagnostics requires bridging the gap between traditional laboratory science and digital transformation.

**Task:** I sought to leverage digital tools to enhance laboratory throughput, reduce manual error, and improve data integrity.

**Action:** I recently upskilled through an AI-mediated Software Engineering Skills Bootcamp, where I applied Python automation and AI/ML concepts directly to scientific workflows. I have authored Python scripts to automate the quantification and visualization of viral marker data from molecular platforms, significantly reducing the time required for technical reporting. **Result:** My "Science-First, Tech-Enhanced" approach has created a force multiplier for diagnostic output. **Situation:** All technical work must be anchored in absolute regulatory compliance, particularly during the current transition from EU IVDR to a GB-specific framework.

**Task:** My objective was to ensure all laboratory assays and reagents met the stringent requirements of ISO 13485, ISO 15189, and GMP.

**Action:** I have managed the end-to-end production of IVDs in CL3 environments, maintaining absolute awareness of IVD standards. I have performed gap analyses to align laboratory SOPs with the latest MHRA and UKAS guidelines. I have direct experience informing the MHRA regarding device performance or safety matters that conflict with manufacturer claims.

**Result:** My deep-seated understanding of IVD regulations ensures that all PHM diagnostic capabilities remain at the forefront of regulatory excellence and patient safety.
