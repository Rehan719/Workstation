**Question:** Please provide evidence of your substantial recent experience working in an accredited clinical diagnostic or reference virology or bacteriology environment, including your role in leading complex projects and maintaining quality standards.

My commitment to quality standards is further evidenced by my tenure as a Senior Laboratory Scientist at the Anthony Nolan Research Institute. I served as the technical lead for UKAS audit preparation, performing granular gap analyses on LIMS workflows and standardizing ABO blood-group testing on the Immunocor Echo platform. My proactive approach to quality management, including the implementation of comprehensive internal audit schedules and corrective actions for ISO 15189 alignment, resulted in the laboratory achieving zero major findings in the 2024 UKAS audit and a 66% reduction in laboratory error rates.

Furthermore, my foundational experience as a Healthcare Scientist Practitioner during the H1N1 pandemic at the UK Health Security Agency (then HPA) involved performing high-volume RT-qPCR diagnostics under CPA standards. I authored emergency SOPs and trained staff to ensure diagnostic throughput during a national public health crisis. Most recently, as a Biotechnologist at Lonza Biologics, I managed technical execution in industrial GMP suites, maintaining ALCOA+ compliant batch records for complex bioprocessing. This multi-sector exposure ensures that I bring a rare combination of regulatory depth, industrial precision, and a deep-rooted understanding of the UKHSA’s mission to the RACU Operations Team.
