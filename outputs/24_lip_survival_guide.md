## 🤝 PART 3: How to Handle ACAS Conciliation

### Before the Call
- **Have these documents in front of you**:
  - `23_acas_tactical_briefing.md` (your script)
  - `02_chronology.md` (for dates)
  - `11_schedule_of_loss.md` (for compensation figures)
- **Prepare your opening statement** (30 seconds max):
  > "Thank you for facilitating this discussion. My case is about discrimination arising from disability. Lonza dismissed me for poor performance, but their own HR records show I was 94% punctual. They never considered how my disability affected my attendance, despite my requests for adjustments. I believe this is unlawful under the Equality Act 2010."

### During the Call
- **Listen first**. Let the conciliator explain the process.
- **Use the "Two Truths" framework** from `18_narrative_framework.md`:
  - Truth 1 (Objective): "Exhibit Q-1 shows 94% punctuality."
  - Truth 2 (Subjective): "Lonza dismissed me for poor performance."
  - Legal consequence: "Under *Thompson v TechFlow*, tribunals must exclude disability factors from performance assessments. Lonza failed this test."
- **If Lonza's representative speaks**, take notes. Do not interrupt. Respond calmly using your script.
- **If asked about settlement**, refer to your range but start high:
  > "Given the strength of the evidence, including Exhibit Q-1 and the procedural failures, I believe a fair settlement would be in the region of £78,000. This reflects the injury to feelings, lost earnings, and the aggravation caused by the non-disclosure of key evidence."

### After the Call
- **Write down what was agreed** and any next steps.
- **Update `10_next_actions.md`** with any new deadlines.
- **If no settlement is reached**, proceed to the tribunal process. Do not be discouraged – most cases do not settle at first conciliation.

## 📬 PART 4: Managing Disclosure and Procedural Steps

### What Is Disclosure?
Disclosure is the legal process where both sides share relevant documents. Lonza must provide documents that support their case OR hurt their case. Your `08_disclosure_request.md` lists what you want from them.

### How to Send the Disclosure Request
1. **Open `08_disclosure_request.md`**.
2. **Copy the entire text**.
3. **Paste into an email** addressed to:
   - Punter Southall Law (Lonza's solicitors) – use the email address from their SAR correspondence
   - CC: ACAS (if conciliation is ongoing)
   - BCC: yourself (for your records)
4. **Subject line**: "Disclosure Request – Minhas v Lonza Biologics Plc – ET Case Reference: [Your Reference]"
5. **Body of email** (brief covering note):
   > Dear Sir/Madam,
   > Please find attached my formal disclosure request pursuant to Rule 31 of the Employment Tribunals Rules of Procedure 2013.
   > I request that you provide the documents listed within 14 days.
   > Should you require any clarification, please do not hesitate to contact me.
   > Yours faithfully,
   > Rehan Minhas (Claimant in Person)

7. **Send the email** and save a copy in your case folder.
