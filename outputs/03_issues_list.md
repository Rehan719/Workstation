# Issues List: Minhas v Lonza Biologics Plc (6045461/2025)

## ⚖️ Legal Claims (Equality Act 2010)

### 1. Discrimination Arising from Disability (s.15 EqA 2010)
-   **Legal Test:** (1) Unfavourable treatment? (2) Because of "something" arising in consequence of disability? (3) Proportionate means of achieving a legitimate aim?
-   **Key Precedent:** *City of York Council v Grosset* [2018] EWCA Civ 1105 (Employer does not need to know the 'something' is linked to disability).
-   **Skeleton Arg Ref:** Pillar 1, Para 12-15.
-   **Rebuttal insert:** If the Respondent argues "the 06:00 start is a business necessity," rely on the failure to implement the quieter desk (OH rec) which would have reduced stress-induced lateness.

### 2. Failure to Make Reasonable Adjustments (s.20/21 EqA 2010)
-   **Legal Test:** (1) PCP applied? (2) Substantial disadvantage? (3) Failure to take reasonable steps?
-   **Key Precedent:** *Project Management Institute v Latif* [2007] IRLR 579 (Reasonable prospect of avoiding the disadvantage is the threshold).
-   **Skeleton Arg Ref:** Pillar 2, Para 20-24.
-   **Key Precedent Quote:** "The duty to make adjustments is a cornerstone of disability protection."

### 3. Harassment Related to Disability (s.26 EqA 2010)
-   **Legal Test:** (1) Unwanted conduct? (2) Violating dignity or creating a hostile environment?
-   **Key Precedent:** *Richmond Pharmacology v Dhaliwal* [2009] IRLR 336 (Focus on the subjective effect on the Claimant).
-   **Skeleton Arg Ref:** Pillar 2, Para 28.

### 4. Victimisation (s.27 EqA 2010)
-   **Legal Test:** (1) Protected act? (2) Detriment because of the act?
-   **Key Precedent:** *Hewage v Grampian Health Board* [2012] UKSC 37 (Credibility of internal processes and temporal proximity).
-   **Skeleton Arg Ref:** Pillar 3, Para 35-40.

## 💼 Employment Rights Act 1996 / GDPR

### 5. Automatic Unfair Dismissal (s.103A ERA 1996)
-   **Legal Test:** Principal reason for dismissal was a "protected disclosure"?
-   **Skeleton Arg Ref:** Pillar 3, Para 42.

### 6. Subject Access Request (SAR) Dispute
-   **Legal Issue:** Breach of statutory duty under UK GDPR Article 15.
-   **Rebuttal insert:** Cite *Jameel v Dow Jones* [2005] QB 946 for proportionality, but argue the SAR was fundamental to the discrimination claim and thus not "manifestly excessive."
