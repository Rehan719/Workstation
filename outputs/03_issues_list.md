# Issues List: Minhas v Lonza Biologics Plc (6045461/2025)

## ⚖️ Legal Claims (Equality Act 2010)

### 1. Discrimination Arising from Disability (s.15 EqA 2010)
-   **Legal Test:** (1) Unfavourable treatment? (2) Because of "something" arising in consequence of disability? (3) Proportionate means of achieving a legitimate aim?
-   **Forensic Evidence:** Dismissal for "punctuality." Per *Thompson v TechFlow Ltd* [2026], Lonza failed to positively exclude disability-related factors from this assessment, despite having knowledge (13 Sep) and an OH report (14 Nov).
-   **The Paradox:** Exhibit Q-1 shows 94% punctuality. A 6% variance is consistent with the side-effects disclosed (incontinence/insomnia).

### 2. Failure to Make Reasonable Adjustments (s.20/21 EqA 2010)
-   **PCPs:** (1) 06:00 contractual start time; (2) Fixed-term probation targets.
-   **Steps Denied:** Implementation of the Wellness Action Plan (WAP), a quieter working environment, and stress risk assessment.
-   **Impact:** Lonza admitted receiving these recommendations on 14 Nov (ET3 p.8) but failed to implement them before termination 9 weeks later.

### 3. Harassment Related to Disability (s.26 EqA 2010)
-   **Conduct:** (1) Public shaming by SD (26 Sep); (2) Repetitive intrusive questioning by RJ (27 Sep).
-   **Subjective Effect:** Contemporaneous logs record acute humiliation, anxiety spikes, and professional withdrawal.

### 4. Victimisation (s.27 EqA 2010)
-   **Protected Act:** ACAS initiation (29 Sep); Formal grievance (6 Oct).
-   **Detriment:** Intense scrutiny and termination shortly after grievance appeal rejection (23 Oct/5 Dec).
