## ⚖️ Legal Claims (Forensic Test Application)

### 1. Whistleblowing (s.103A ERA 1996)
-   **Legal Test:** (1) Did the Claimant make a protected disclosure? (2) Was the principal reason for dismissal the disclosure?
-   **Public Interest Test:** Satisfied under *Chesterton Global Ltd v Nurmohamed* [2017]. Sterile pharmaceutical manufacturing is a matter of critical public safety.
-   **Forensic Evidence:** Disclosures regarding clean-room protocol failures (Oct/Nov 2025). The temporal proximity to dismissal and the contradictory performance metrics support retaliatory motive.

### 2. Discrimination Arising from Disability (s.15 EqA 2010)
-   **Thompson-scrutiny:** Under *Thompson v TechFlow Ltd* [2026] EAT 12, Lonza was required to positively exclude disability factors (insomnia/anxiety side-effects) from the attendance assessment.
-   **Forensic Evidence:** Lonza's own data (Exhibit Q-1) shows 94% compliance. Dismissal for a 6% variance—without implementing adjustments—is discriminatory.

### 3. Failure to Adjust (s.20/21 EqA)
-   **Forensic Evidence:** Admission in ET3 (p.8, para 16) that Dr. Tahir (OH) recommended a quieter working environment and WAP on 14 Nov 2025. Lonza failed to implement these before termination on 21 Jan.
