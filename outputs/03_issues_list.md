### 1. Discrimination Arising from Disability (s.15 EqA 2010)
-   **Legal Test:** (1) Did the Respondent treat the Claimant unfavourably? (2) Was this because of "something" arising in consequence of the Claimant's disability?
-   **Case Law:** *Thompson v TechFlow Ltd* [2026] EAT 12 (tribunals must positively exclude disability factors from performance assessments).
-   **Forensic Evidence:** Dismissal on 21 Jan 2026 for "poor attendance." Exhibit Q-1 shows a 94% punctuality rate. The 6% variance is consistent with side-effects (insomnia/incontinence) disclosed on 13 Sep 2025.
-   **Key Precedent Quote:** "Scrutiny of performance metrics must be granular in the context of disability."
