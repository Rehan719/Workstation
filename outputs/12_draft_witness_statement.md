# Skeleton Witness Statement: Minhas v Lonza Biologics Plc (6045461/2025)

## ⚖️ Draft Statement of Rehan Minhas (Forensic Detail)

### 1. Introduction
1.1  I, Rehan Arshad Minhas, of 66C Chandos Crescent, Edgware, HA8 6HL, state that the following is a true and accurate account of my employment with Lonza Biologics Plc from 27 June 2025 until my dismissal on 21 January 2026.

### 2. Early Employment & Health Reporting (August - September 2025)
2.1  I commenced my role as a Biotechnologist in good faith. In late August and early September 2025, I began experiencing episodic medication side effects, including urgent toilet needs and incontinence.
2.2  Following Lonza's Sickness Absence Policy, I reported these incidents via the UKSL 425 process on 5 separate occasions to my manager, SD.
2.3  Instead of support or an OH referral, SD began questioning my health in public. I was told I was "off-plant every other day," despite my documented attendance and the nature of my reported condition.

### 3. Disability Disclosure & Data Breach (13 September 2025)
3.1  On 13 September 2025 at approximately 14:00, I formally disclosed my mental health condition and side effects to my line manager, RJ. I explained that I suffered from insomnia and anxiety, exacerbated by the medication.
3.2  Immediately following this disclosure, RJ extended my probation to 6 months and imposed targets (95% CSOD reads) which were significantly higher than the standard 90% required of my peers.
3.3  I later discovered that RJ recorded my sensitive health data in an unprotected Excel worksheet on a shared drive, accessible to unauthorized personnel. This was a clear breach of GDPR Article 9 and Clause 14 of my contract.

### 4. Coordinated Harassment & Public Shaming (September 2025)
4.1  On 26 September 2025 at 07:45, SD publicly shamed me in an open-plan office for a 20-minute lateness. This lateness was a direct consequence of the insomnia I had disclosed on 13 September.
4.2  On 27 September 2025, I was asked "Are you OK?" approximately 16 times by managers and colleagues. This repetition created a "social labeling" effect, making me feel isolated and "unwell" rather than a professional colleague. When I complained to RJ, he dismissively told me "you can do what you want."

### 5. Health Crisis & Protected Act (October 2025)
5.1  The stress of this hostile environment led to a severe psychiatric deterioration. On 3 October 2025, I was forced to contact the NHS Crisis Team (Northwick Park/CNWL) for urgent intervention.
5.2  On 6 October 2025, I submitted a formal grievance. I believe this was a protected act under s.27 EqA. The Respondent's reaction was further defensiveness rather than investigation.

### 6. Patient Safety Disclosures (October - November 2025)
6.1  During my grievance and subsequent meetings, I raised "Clean-Room Risks" regarding procedural failures in the manufacturing Suites that compromised product sterility.
6.2  Under *Chesterton Global Ltd v Nurmohamed* [2017], these were protected disclosures made in the public interest. Instead of investigating these risks, Lonza intensified performance monitoring against me.

### 7. The Punctuality Paradox (Exhibit Q-1)
7.1  I was shocked to be dismissed on 21 January 2026 for "poor attendance." I have since seen **Exhibit Q-1**, Lonza's own HR records, which show I was punctual for 94% of my shifts.
7.2  A 6% variance is entirely consistent with the medical condition I disclosed. Lonza failed to implement the OH-recommended adjustments (quiet desk, advanced agendas) which would have mitigated even this small variance.

### 8. Conclusion
8.1  I believe my termination was not a genuine assessment of capability, but a retaliatory act for my disability disclosure and my patient safety concerns. Lonza chose to label 94% compliance as failure to justify the removal of a whistleblower.
