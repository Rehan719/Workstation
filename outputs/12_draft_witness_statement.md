## ⚖️ Draft Skeleton Witness Statement (24 May 2024)

### 1. Introduction
1.1  I, Rehan Arshad Minhas, of 66C Chandos Crescent, Edgware, HA8 6HL, state that the following is a true and accurate account of the events during my employment with Lonza Biologics Plc from 27 June 2025 until my dismissal on 21 January 2026.

### 2. Disability Disclosure (13 September 2025)
2.1  On 13 September 2025, I formally disclosed to my line manager, "RJ," that I suffered from a long-term mental health condition and that my medication caused side effects including insomnia, anxiety, and incontinence.
2.2  Following this disclosure, RJ extended my probation. I later discovered that RJ had recorded my sensitive health data in an unprotected Excel file accessible to other staff members.

### 3. Harassment and Public Shaming (26-27 September 2025)
3.1  On 26 September 2025 at approximately 07:45, "SD" (Shift Manager) publicly shamed me in an open-plan office for being 20 minutes late. This interaction took place in front of colleagues and was deeply humiliating.
3.2  On 27 September 2025, I was asked "Are you OK?" approximately 16 times by managers and colleagues. When I attempted to raise this as a concern with RJ, he refused to investigate, stating "you can do what you want."
3.3  My notes from the meeting on 27 September 2025, documented in `English.docx`, record the names of witnesses TNO, ADY, and SAV who were present during these interactions.

### 4. Health Crisis and Protected Act (October 2025)
4.1  The stress of these interactions led to a severe deterioration in my mental health, culminating in an intervention by the NHS Crisis Team (Northwick Park/CNWL) on 3 October 2025.
4.2  On 6 October 2025, I submitted a formal grievance alleging disability discrimination, harassment, and victimisation. This followed my initiation of ACAS Early Conciliation on 29 September 2025.

### 5. Subject Access Request (SAR) & Legal defensiveness
5.1  On 14 October 2025, I received a letter from Lonza's solicitors, Punter Southall Law, regarding my Subject Access Request.
5.2  In that letter, they challenged my request as "manifestly excessive" and mentioned a 30-day CCTV retention policy, implying that the footage of my harassment might already be deleted. This legalistic and defensive approach added significantly to my distress.

### 6. Failure to Make Reasonable Adjustments (November 2025 - January 2026)
6.1  Following an Occupational Health (OH) assessment on 13 November 2025, Dr. Tahir recommended a Wellness Action Plan (WAP), a stress risk assessment, and a quieter working environment.
6.2  Despite these recommendations, and despite my ongoing health challenges, the Respondent failed to implement a WAP or conduct a formal stress risk assessment before my dismissal on 21 January 2026.

### 7. Dismissal and Victimisation (21 January 2026)
7.1  On 21 January 2026, my employment was terminated for failing to pass probation. The reasons cited were "punctuality" and "failure to meet probation targets."
7.2  I believe the real reason for my dismissal was my disability and the fact that I had raised a formal grievance against management. The timing of my dismissal, shortly after my grievance appeal was rejected on 23 October 2025 and again on 5 December 2025, confirms this.

### 8. Conclusion
8.1  I believe that the Respondent's actions have caused me significant psychiatric harm and have unfairly ended my career with Lonza Biologics Plc.
