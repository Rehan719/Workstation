# Skeleton Witness Statement: Minhas v Lonza Biologics Plc (6045461/2025)

## ⚖️ First-Person Narrative of Rehan Arshad Minhas (Forensic-Active)

### 1. Introduction & Background
1.1  I, Rehan Arshad Minhas, of 66C Chandos Crescent, Edgware, HA8 6HL, am a qualified Biotechnologist. I commenced employment with Lonza Biologics Plc (the "Respondent") on 27 June 2025. I am a disabled person within the meaning of s.6 EqA 2010.

### 2. Early Incidents & Reporting (August – September 2025)
2.1  In late August 2025, I began experiencing significant side-effects from medication for my long-term condition. These included intermittent incontinence and urgent toilet needs.
2.2  I followed the Respondent's **Sickness Absence Policy (Dec 2016)**, reporting 5 separate incidents via the **UKSL 425 process** to SD (Plant Manager).
2.3  Instead of triggering an OH referral, SD met my reports with suspicion, claiming I was "off-plant every other day." This reframed my medical needs as performance failure.

### 3. Disability Disclosure & Probation (13 September 2025)
3.1  On 13 September 2025 at 14:00, I formally disclosed my condition and side effects to RJ (Line Manager). I explained my struggle with insomnia and anxiety.
3.2  The response was immediate and punitive: RJ extended my probation to 6 months and imposed a **95% CSOD read target**, while peers were held only to 90%.
3.3  RJ also recorded my sensitive health data in an **unprotected Excel worksheet** on a shared drive, a devastating breach of GDPR and Clause 14 of my contract.

### 4. Coordinated Harassment (September 2025)
4.1  On 26 September 2025 at 07:45, SD publicly shamed me in an open-plan office for a 20-min lateness caused by insomnia.
4.2  On 27 September 2025, I was asked "Are you OK?" approximately 16 times. This repetition isolated me. When I raised this to RJ, he dismissively said "you can do what you want."

### 5. Health Crisis & Whistleblowing (October – November 2025)
5.1  On 3 October 2025, the stress led to a severe mental health crisis, requiring **NHS Crisis Team (Northwick Park/CNWL)** intervention.
5.2  On 6 October, I submitted a formal grievance. During subsequent meetings, I also raised **"Clean-Room Risks"** concerning sterility procedural failures (see `English.docx`). These disclosures were in the public interest.

### 6. The Punctuality Paradox (Exhibit Q-1)
6.1  I was dismissed on 21 January 2026 for "poor attendance." However, **Exhibit Q-1** (Lonza's own metrics) confirms I was punctual for **94% of my shifts**.
6.2  Lonza failed to implement the OH-recommended adjustments from 14 Nov 2025 (quiet desk, WAP). Under **Thompson v TechFlow Ltd [2026]**, they were required to exclude my disability from this assessment. They did not.
