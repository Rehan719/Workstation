-   "The question seems to contradict Exhibit Q-1; may I refer to that document before answering?"

-   **Anchor 1:** 94% Punctuality (Objective Truth).
-   **Anchor 2:** 14 Nov OH Report (The Roadmap they ignored).

-   If evidence is challenged: State, "This is based on my contemporaneous log written at the time."
