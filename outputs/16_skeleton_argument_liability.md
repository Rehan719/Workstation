# Skeleton Argument: Liability Hearing (Minhas v Lonza)

## ⚖️ The Tripartite Pillar Framework

### Pillar 1: Discrimination Arising from Disability (s.15 EqA)
1.  The Respondent dismissed the Claimant for "punctuality" and "failure to meet targets."
2.  The Claimant’s lateness was a direct consequence of insomnia and anxiety (Symptoms).
3.  Per *City of York Council v Grosset* [2018], the Respondent’s lack of belief that the lateness was linked to disability is no defense. The link is established by the GP Letter (30 Sep 2025).

### Pillar 2: Procedural Failure & Duty to Adjust (s.20-21 EqA)
4.  The Respondent admitted receiving OH recommendations on 14 Nov 2025.
5.  Failure to implement a Wellness Action Plan (WAP) or Stress Risk Assessment before the 21 Jan termination is a clear breach of s.21.
6.  *Project Management Institute v Latif* [2007] establishes that these steps had a real prospect of avoiding the disadvantage.

### Pillar 3: Victimisation (s.27 EqA)
7.  The Claimant performed a protected act on 6 Oct 2025 (Grievance).
8.  The subsequent termination on 21 Jan 2026, following the rejection of his appeal, satisfies the "detriment" and "causation" tests.
9.  *Hewage v Grampian Health Board* [2012] supports an inference of discrimination given the temporal proximity and procedural flaws.
