# Skeleton Argument: Liability Hearing (Minhas v Lonza)

## ⚖️ The Two Truths Framework

### Introduction
1.  There are two truths in this case.
2.  **Truth One:** The Respondent's own HR records (Exhibit Q-1) show Rehan Minhas was 94% punctual.
3.  **Truth Two:** The Respondent dismissed him for "poor attendance."
4.  The Tribunal is invited to conclude that the dismissal was not a factual assessment, but a subjective fiction designed to mask discrimination and victimisation.

### Pillar 1: Discrimination (s.15 EqA)
5.  Exhibit Q-1 proves compliance. The "Something Arising" (6% lateness) was a symptom of disability-related stress/insomnia, which *Grosset* confirms is the Respondent's risk to manage.

### Pillar 2: Failure to Adjust (s.20-21 EqA)
6.  The Respondent had the "Objective Truth" (OH report) but failed to act. As in *Latif*, the steps (quieter desk, WAP) had a real prospect of success.

### Pillar 3: Victimisation (s.27 EqA)
7.  The termination was the "Subjective Conclusion" to the Protected Act (Grievance). *Hewage* supports an inference of discrimination given the data contradiction.
