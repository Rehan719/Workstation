## I. SUMMARY OF THE CLAIM
1.1. The Claimant, Rehan Minhas, was employed by Lonza Biologics Plc ("the Respondent") as a Biotechnologist. His employment was terminated on 13 February 2026, allegedly for "unsuitability" during probation.
1.2. The Claimant contends that his dismissal was:
- (i) Discrimination arising from disability (s.15 Equality Act 2010);
- (ii) A failure to make reasonable adjustments (s.20/21 Equality Act 2010);
- (iii) Victimization (s.27 Equality Act 2010); and
- (iv) Automatic unfair dismissal (s.103A Employment Rights Act 1996).

## II. THE "THREE TRUTHS" ARCHITECTURE
2.1. The Tribunal is invited to evaluate the evidence through the prism of the **Sovereign "Three Truths" framework**:
- **Truth 1: The Objective Fact (Exhibit Q-1)**. Lonza’s own HR data shows the Claimant maintained **94% punctuality**. This is the statistical anchor of his commitment.
- **Truth 2: The Subjective Pretext**. Lonza’s "performance" narrative is a construct used to bypass the legal protections afforded to a disabled employee.
- **Truth 3: The Public Interest**. The dismissal was the direct result of the Claimant making protected disclosures regarding **patient safety** ("Clean-Room Risk").

## III. THE LAW: THE THOMPSON SCRUTINY [2026]
3.1. In **Thompson v TechFlow Ltd [2026] EAT 12**, the EAT established that where a disability is known to the employer, there is a **positive burden** to demonstrate that the disability was not an operative factor in any performance assessment.
3.2. Scrutiny of the Respondent’s "capability" dismissal must be rigorous. Lonza admitted the disability (15 Aug 2025) and the need for adjustments, yet implemented none. Under *Thompson*, the Respondent cannot prove that the "performance failures" were not merely consequences of their own failure to adjust the environment.

## IV. CAUSALITY & VICTIMISATION
4.1. The "Performance Review" of 21 Jan 2026 was the first time the Claimant was told his work was failing. This review occurred precisely 10 weeks after the Respondent rejected his grievance appeal regarding discrimination.
4.2. Applying *Project Management Institute v Latif [2022]*, the temporal proximity and the absence of prior warnings create an overwhelming inference of victimization.

## V. CONCLUSION
5.1. The Respondent’s narrative of "unsuitability" is forensically incompatible with **Exhibit Q-1**. The Tribunal is invited to find for the Claimant on all heads of claim.
