## 8.2 SUBSTANTIVE GROUNDS OF CLAIM (INCIDENT-LEVEL DETAIL)

### A. DISCRIMINATION ARISING FROM DISABILITY (s.15 EqA 2010)
The Respondent treated the Claimant unfavourably because of something arising in consequence of his disability (mental health condition and medication side-effects, specifically episodic diarrhoea and anxiety).

**Key Incidents:**
1. **Late August – Early September 2025:** Claimant reported 2-3 episodes of diarrhoea via UKSL 425 to SD. No OH referral or adjustments followed. Managers RJ and SD began public shaming, asserting Claimant was "off-plant every other day." [Minhas_Contemporaneous_Log_6Oct20252.pdf, p.1]
2. **13 September 2025:** Following a confidential disclosure of his disability, RJ immediately extended the Claimant’s probation to six months and imposed onerous targets not required of peers (95% CSOD completion). [Minhas_Contemporaneous_Log_6Oct20252.pdf, p.3]
3. **26 September 2025:** SD publicly admonished the Claimant in an open-plan office for a 20-minute lateness caused by a disability-related health episode reported earlier that day. Peers (TNO, ADY) were not shamed for similar lateness. [Minhas_Contemporaneous_Log_6Oct20252.pdf, p.4]
4. **94% Punctuality Contradiction:** Internal HR logs (Exhibit Q-1) show a 94% punctuality rate, yet the Respondent used "attendance" as the pretext for dismissal, disregarding the disability link to the remaining 6%.

### B. FAILURE TO MAKE REASONABLE ADJUSTMENTS (s.20/21 EqA 2010)
The Respondent applied PCPs (requirement to maintain fixed start times and plant presence) that put the Claimant at a substantial disadvantage.

**Key Incidents:**
1. **14 November 2025:** Claimant requested flexible start times due to disability-related morning fatigue [Minhas_Contemporaneous_Log_6Oct20252.pdf, p.3]. No formal response or implementation followed.
2. **Disregard of OH Recommendations:** Despite OH recommending a Stress Risk Assessment and Wellness Action Plan (13 Nov 2025), the Respondent delayed completion until 8 Jan 2026, mere days before termination. [13.02.2026 RM Outcome Letter 1 SIGNED.pdf, p.1]

### C. VICTIMISATION (s.27 EqA 2010)
The Claimant was subjected to detriments (intensified monitoring and dismissal) after performing protected acts (Formal Grievance, 6 Oct 2025).

**Key Incidents:**
1. **27 September 2025:** RJ subjected the Claimant to "16 queries" in a single session regarding minor movements, shortly after the Claimant raised concerns about data protection (unsecured health Excel file). [Minhas_Contemporaneous_Log_6Oct20252.pdf, p.5]
2. **Temporal Proximity:** Dismissal was confirmed on 21 Jan 2026, shortly after the Claimant persisted in seeking feedback and challenging the lack of adjustments.

### D. AUTOMATIC UNFAIR DISMISSAL (s.103A ERA 1996)
The principal reason for dismissal was the Claimant’s protected disclosures regarding patient safety in a clean-room environment.

**Key Incidents:**
1. **28 October & 13 November 2025:** Claimant submitted reports regarding patient safety and compliance risks. [13.02.2026 RM Outcome Letter 1 SIGNED.pdf, p.2]
2. **Retaliatory Dismissal:** The Respondent moved to terminate employment under the pretext of "probation failure" despite the Claimant's high technical competence (filtration/buffer tasks completed without incident).

## HEALTH IMPACT NARRATIVE
The Respondent’s conduct caused a catastrophic deterioration in the Claimant’s mental health, leading to a psychiatric crisis and NHS crisis team involvement on **3 October 2025**. Discrimination became more aggressive as the Claimant's health declined. [Minhas_Contemporaneous_Log_6Oct20252.pdf, p.6]
