# Legal Compliance Guide (UKHSA Interview)

## 1. Equality Act 2010
- Relevant for: Competency assessments and accessible training documentation.
- Action: Mention inclusive training materials and non-discriminatory assessment criteria.

## 2. GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018
- Relevant for: Handling patient samples and clinical trial data.
- Action: Emphasise ALCOA+ principles, anonymisation, and secure data transfer (AES-256).

## 3. Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984
- Relevant for: Statutory notifications and UKHSA's mandate.
- Action: Link surveillance activities to the statutory duty to protect public health.
