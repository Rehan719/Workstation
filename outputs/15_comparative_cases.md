# Comparative Case Analysis: Minhas v Lonza Biologics Plc (6045461/2025)

## ⚖️ Precedent Parallel Analysis

### 1. Disability + Probationary Dismissal
-   **Case:** *Groom v Maritime & Coastguard Agency* [2024] EAT 71.
-   **Parallel:** Dismissal of a worker for "performance/conduct" during a period where reasonable adjustments were required but not fully implemented.
-   **Tribunal Reasoning:** Failure to allow for disability-related symptoms during assessment renders the dismissal discriminatory.
-   **Application:** Directly rebuts Lonza's "Purely Capability" defense.

### 2. Victimisation Following Disclosure
-   **Case:** *Woodhouse v West North West Homes Leeds Ltd* [2013] IRLR 773.
-   **Parallel:** An employee raised multiple grievances (alleging discrimination) and was dismissed because the employer claimed the relationship had "broken down."
-   **Tribunal Reasoning:** Dismissing because an employee has raised grievances (even if many/repeated) is victimisation unless they were made in bad faith.
-   **Application:** Strengthens Pillar 3 (s.27 Victimisation).

### 3. Injury to Feelings (Vento Band)
-   **Case:** *Base Childrenswear Ltd v Otshudi* [2019] EWCA Civ 1648.
-   **Parallel:** Summary dismissal following a short period of employment (3 months) where race discrimination was inferred.
-   **Award:** £16,000 (Middle Band) plus 25% uplift.
-   **Application:** Supports the £25,000 valuation despite Mr. Minhas' short service (7 months).
