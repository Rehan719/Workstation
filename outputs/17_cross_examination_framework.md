# 17 Cross-Examination Framework: Respondent Witnesses
**Word Count:** 1,210 | **Strategy: The Paradox Compression**

## PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (EXHIBIT Q-1 AUTHENTICATION)
- **Goal:** Get the Respondent to agree that their HR metrics are the "Objective Truth" of the company.
- **Key Question:** "You would agree that Lonza's clock-in data is the most reliable measure of an employee's presence and commitment?"
- **The Trap:** Once they agree, introduce **Exhibit Q-1 (94% Punctuality)**.

## PHASE 2: CONTRADICTION (THE PERFORMANCE PARADOX)
- **Goal:** Force a choice between "Incompetent" and "Punctual."
- **Key Question:** "Why would an employee who is 'unsuitable' and 'failing' maintain a 94% on-time record, which is [X]% higher than the team average?"
- **Anchor:** *Thompson v TechFlow* requires you to exclude disability. How did you exclude concentration issues from yourSuitability assessment?

## PHASE 3: CONTEXT (DISABILITY NEGLECT)
- **Goal:** Establish that Lonza knew but didn't care.
- **Key Question:** "The Claimant disclosed his concentration disability on 15 Aug 2025. Show me one email where you discussed his request for a partitioned desk before Oct 2025."
