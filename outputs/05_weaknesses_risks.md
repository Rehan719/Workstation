| **7. SAR Procedural Delay** | High | Med | The Respondent's solicitors (Punter Southall Law) challenging the SAR as "manifestly excessive" could delay the disclosure of the Excel file and CCTV. Counter with GDPR Recital 63 (the purpose of SAR is to verify lawfulness). |
| **8. CCTV Deletion Risk** | High | High | The 30-day CCTV deletion policy mentioned in the SAR letter means footage for late September 2025 may have been deleted before the 6 October legal hold was applied. Request an urgent meeting on evidence preservation. |

## 📉 Strategic Risk Mitigation
-   **Discovery Focused:** The strongest mitigation for almost every risk is the "Discovery of the unprotected Excel file" and "Internal Emails" (see `08_disclosure_request.md`).
-   **Medical Context:** Proving the s.15 link (that lateness/targets arose *from* the disability) is critical. Secure the full NHS Crisis Team notes (Oct 2025) and OH Report (Nov 2025) immediately.
