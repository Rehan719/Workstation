# Weaknesses & Risks: Minhas v Lonza Biologics Plc (6045461/2025)

## ⚠️ Weaknesses & Risks

### 1. Probationary Status (Capability Defense)
-   The Respondent's core defense is that the Claimant failed his "contractual probationary period" due to non-compliance with "objective targets" (Akta/IPF sign-off) and "punctuality."
-   **Risk:** The Tribunal may find that the dismissal was for a "fair reason" (capability/conduct) and that the Respondent's procedure (meetings on 4 Jan, 21 Jan) was sufficient for a new employee.

### 2. Disputed Evidence (Harassment)
-   The Respondent denies all allegations of "public shaming" and "intense scrutiny" (ET3, p.10, para 20).
-   **Risk:** Without independent witness testimony (e.g., from TNO, ADY, SAV, RB), the Tribunal will have to choose between the Claimant’s and Respondent’s conflicting accounts. The Respondent's request for "Further and Better Particulars" is a strategic move to catch the Claimant in inconsistencies.

### 3. Knowledge of Disability (Timing)
-   The Respondent admits disclosure on 13 Sep 2025, but may argue that earlier "lateness" or "performance" issues (Aug/Sep) were unrelated to the disability.
-   **Risk:** If the Respondent can show that the performance concerns predated the disclosure, they may argue that the disability was a later "excuse" for pre-existing failure.

### 4. Occupational Health (OH) Adherence
-   The Respondent claims (ET3, p.8, para 16) that it *did* make referrals and that the Claimant failed to attend an initial appointment (27 Aug 2025).
-   **Risk:** This could be used to show that the Respondent was "trying" to help but the Claimant was "uncooperative" or "obstructive," potentially reducing any award for failure to make adjustments.

### 5. Witness Credibility (Internal)
-   The Respondent’s witnesses (RJ, SD, SKM, Nic Murrell) are still employed and likely well-prepared. Their internal notes/statements will likely support the "fair dismissal" narrative.
-   **Risk:** The Claimant is self-represented or using a smaller legal team, while the Respondent is represented by "Draper Lang LLP," a sophisticated firm.

### 6. Compensation Caps & Mitigation
-   The Claimant was only employed from Jun 2025 to Jan 2026 (approx. 7 months).
-   **Risk:** Any award for "loss of earnings" will be capped by the Claimant’s duty to mitigate their loss (i.e., by finding a new job). The Tribunal may find that a 7-month tenure does not warrant a significant future loss of earnings award.

### 7. Complexity of "s.15" Claims
-   "Discrimination arising from disability" requires the Claimant to prove that the "something" (e.g., lateness) arose *from* the disability.
-   **Risk:** Proving that insomnia or anxiety *always* causes 06:00 lateness is legally and medically complex and will require expert medical evidence.
