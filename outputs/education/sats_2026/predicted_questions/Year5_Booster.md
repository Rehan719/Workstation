## 1. MATHEMATICS: NUMBER & PLACE VALUE

## 2. MATHEMATICS: FRACTIONS, DECIMALS & PERCENTAGES
- **Equivalent Fractions:** Find three fractions equivalent to 3/5 (6/10, 30/50, 60/100).

## 3. ENGLISH GPS: VOCABULARY & PUNCTUATION
- **Relative Clauses:** Rewrite: "The Arsenal fans cheered. They were happy." using *who*.

- **Modal Verbs:** Identify the modal verb: "The rocket *might* reach Mars by July."
- **Parenthesis:** Use brackets for extra information: "Bukayo Saka (the Arsenal winger) scored a goal."
