# Executive Summary: Minhas v Lonza Biologics Plc (6045461/2025)

## ⚖️ Case Overview
*"There are two truths in this case. Truth One: the Respondent's own HR records (Exhibit Q-1) show Rehan Minhas was 94% punctual. Truth Two: the Respondent dismissed him for poor attendance. The tribunal's task is to decide which truth governs."*

## 🗝️ Key Findings
1.  **Evidentiary Linchpin (Exhibit Q-1):** Internal attendance metrics confirm 94% compliance. The dismissal for "punctuality" is therefore objectively contradicted by the Respondent's own data.
2.  **Victimisation (s.27 EqA 2010):** Strongest claim. Rejection of appeal (23 Oct) and subsequent termination shortly after grievance confirms a campaign of retaliation.
3.  **Failure to Make Reasonable Adjustments:** No evidence of implementing the OH-recommended Wellness Action Plan (14 Nov 2025).
