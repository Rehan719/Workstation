# UK Legal Precision Engine (UKLPE)

The organism enforces native alignment with UK statutes. Decisions are audited against `config/legal_precision.yaml`.

## 📜 Key Encoded Statutes
- **Equality Act 2010**: Direct discrimination (s.13) and Disability adjustments (s.20) are blocked at the GaaS intercept.
- **ERA 1996**: Fair dismissal (s.98) and Whistleblowing (s.103A) detection.
- **GDPR**: Article 22 checks for automated decision-making triggers.
- **Taxation**: IR35 and VAT threshold monitoring for VSB transactions.

## 🔗 Traceability
Each legal audit produces a SHA-3-512 trace hash stored in the UEG, providing court-admissible proof of constitutional reasoning.
