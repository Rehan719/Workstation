# UK Legal Precision Engine (UKLPE) Compliance

## ⚖️ Scope
The v17.0 organism is natively aligned with UK law across four key domains:

### 1. Employment Law
- **Equality Act 2010**: Automated detection of Direct (s.13) and Indirect (s.19) discrimination.
- **Disability Discrimination (s.15)**: Integrated checks for reasonable adjustments (s.20).
- **ERA 1996**: Fair dismissal procedures (s.98) and Whistleblowing protection (s.103A).
- **ACAS**: Compliance with the Code of Practice on Disciplinary and Grievance procedures.

### 2. Civil Procedure Rules (CPR)
- **Overriding Objective (CPR 1.1)**: Ensuring proportionality and justice.
- **Summary Judgment (Part 24)**: Early case assessment logic.
- **Limitation Act 1980**: Statutory clock monitoring for claims.

### 3. Taxation
- **IR35 (ITEPA 2003)**: Status determination for off-payroll workers.
- **VATA 1994**: Mandatory registration threshold monitoring.
- **GAAR**: General Anti-Abuse Rule enforcement.

### 4. GDPR (Data Protection)
- **Lawfulness & Transparency (Art 5/6)**: Mandatory legal basis checks.
- **Automated Decision-making (Art 22)**: Human-in-the-loop triggers.
