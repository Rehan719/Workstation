# Sexta-Veritas Care Framework

The **Sexta-Veritas** framework is a six-dimensional truth model applied to Health & Social Care. It ensures that care pathways are ethical, predictive, and patient-centered.

## 🧬 The Six Truths of Care

1.  **Objective (Health Records)**: Clinical data, vital signs (NEWS2), medication history, and formal diagnoses.
2.  **Subjective (Patient Voice)**: Patient preferences, lived experience, qualitative feedback, and personal goals.
3.  **Procedural (Care Pathways)**: Alignment with CQC standards, Care Act 2014, and NICE guidelines.
4.  **Temporal (Long-term Outcomes)**: Tracking deterioration or improvement over years, not just days.
5.  **Predictive (Risk Forecasting)**: Using AI to forecast falls, sepsis, or caregiver burnout.
6.  **Ethical (Equity & Justice)**: Ensuring equitable access and safeguarding the most vulnerable.
