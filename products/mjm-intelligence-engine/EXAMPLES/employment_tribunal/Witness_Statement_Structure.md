# Witness Statement Guidance

## Structure of a Witness Statement
A good witness statement should be:
1. **Chronological:** Follow the order of events.
2. **Numbered:** Each paragraph should have a unique number.
3. **Evidence-based:** Refer to specific documents (exhibits) where possible.
4. **First-person:** "I saw...", "I heard...", "I received...".

## Statement of Truth
At the end of your statement, you MUST include:
*"I believe that the facts stated in this witness statement are true. I understand that proceedings for contempt of court may be brought against anyone who makes, or causes to be made, a false statement in a document verified by a statement of truth without an honest belief in its truth."*

## Reference: Employment Tribunal Rules 2013, Rule 73
Witness statements are usually taken 'as read' at the hearing, but you may be cross-examined on the contents.
