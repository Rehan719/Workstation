# ACAS Early Conciliation Pack

## Overview
Before submitting a claim to an Employment Tribunal, you must usually notify ACAS that you intend to do so. This triggers 'Early Conciliation'.

## Key Information
- **Legislation:** Section 18A Employment Tribunals Act 1996
- **Requirement:** Certificate Number from ACAS must be entered on the ET1 form.
- **Deadline Extension:** Conciliation pauses the limitation period (usually 3 months).

## Action Checklist
1. [ ] Identify the correct legal name of the Respondent.
2. [ ] Submit the Early Conciliation form on the ACAS website.
3. [ ] Keep the ACAS Certificate safe; it is a mandatory requirement for ET1.
4. [ ] Note the date conciliation started and ended to calculate the new Tribunal deadline.
