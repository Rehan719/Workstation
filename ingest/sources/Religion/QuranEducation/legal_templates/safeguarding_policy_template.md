# Quran Education Platform: Safeguarding Policy v1.0
## Domain: RELIGION::QEP::LAW

### 1. Introduction
The Quran Education Platform (QEP) is committed to providing a safe and secure environment for all students, particularly children and vulnerable adults.

### 2. Policy Statement
All staff, volunteers, and teachers are required to adhere to the highest standards of child protection and safeguarding.

### 3. Recruitment and Training
- All teachers must undergo a Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check (or equivalent).
- All staff must complete mandatory safeguarding training.
- Ijazah chains are verified for theological integrity and historical conduct.

### 4. Reporting Procedures
Any concerns regarding the safety or wellbeing of a student must be reported immediately to the Safeguarding Lead.

### 5. Monitoring and Review
This policy is reviewed quarterly by the Scholar Board and Legal Domain.
