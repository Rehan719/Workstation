# v149.0 Universal Access & Domain Audit

## 1. Executive Summary
This document outlines the mapping of existing Workstation tools, self-improvement mechanisms, and civilizational infrastructure to the five great domains of human endeavor: Religion, Science, Law, Employment, and Education. v149.0 represents the "Universal Empowerment" epoch, making these capabilities free, accessible, and playful for all.

## 2. Tool & Capability Mapping

### 2.1 Domain: Religion
*   **Existing Tools**: Knowledge Hub (CoE), AI CEO (Ethics), UVAID (Scripture Analysis), Extrospection (Global Faith Trends).
*   **Specialized Reactor**: **Religion Reactor** – Pre-loaded with sacred texts, theological commentaries, and interfaith dialogue corpora.
*   **v149.0 Integration**: AI-assisted scripture study, interfaith dialogue rooms, ethical AI tools (Article-based), community building modules.

### 2.2 Domain: Science
*   **Existing Tools**: Quad Synthesis Reactor (Discovery Engine), UVAID (Data Visualization), QEPEngine (Orchestration), Simulation (Experimentation).
*   **Specialized Reactor**: **Science Reactor** – Integrated with arXiv, PubMed (simulated), and planetary research data.
*   **v149.0 Integration**: Research assistants, experiment simulation, data analysis pipelines, planetary collaboration networks.

### 2.3 Domain: Law
*   **Existing Tools**: Constitutional Agent, Introspection (Precedent), Governance Portal (Treaty Studio), PQC Security (Digital Sovereignty).
*   **Specialized Reactor**: **Law Reactor** – Pre-loaded with legal databases, case law, and international regulatory frameworks.
*   **v149.0 Integration**: Legal research, contract generator, regulatory compliance monitor, AI-mediated dispute resolution.

### 2.4 Domain: Employment
*   **Existing Tools**: BTO Catalog (Infrastructure), VSB Simulation (Business Model), C-Suite (Executive Mentorship), Marketplace.
*   **Specialized Reactor**: **Employment Reactor** – Pre-loaded with job market data, skill frameworks, and business plan templates.
*   **v149.0 Integration**: Career assessment, skill-building courses, job matching, VSB business simulation (entrepreneurial incubator).

### 2.5 Domain: Education
*   **Existing Tools**: Knowledge Hub, Learning Dashboard (v146.0), Digital Reactors (Incubators), UVIAP (Knowledge Ingestion).
*   **Specialized Reactor**: **Education Reactor** – Pre-loaded with curriculum standards, learning object repositories, and assessment tools.
*   **v149.0 Integration**: Personalized learning paths, interactive lessons, progress tracking, project-based reactor incubators.

## 3. Self-Improvement Convergence
All users will now have visibility and influence over:
- **Evolution Log**: System-generated proposals for domain-specific improvements.
- **Voting Interface**: Community-driven governance.
- **Impact Dashboard**: Visualization of individual and collective contribution to system fidelity.
- **Constitutional Agent**: Universal guardian for domain-specific queries.

## 4. Technical Strategy
- **Persistence**: Gamification (XP, Badges) stored in SQLite/JSON linked to Citizen Passport DID.
- **AI Models**: Default to Llama 3.2 3B via Ollama. Settings page includes a "Model Manager" for one-click pulls.
- **UX Layer**:
  - **Animation**: Framer Motion for fluid transitions.
  - **Celebration**: tsParticles for achievement confetti.
  - **Audio**: Howler.js for subtle interface chimes (opt-out).
  - **Onboarding**: React Joyride for narrative-driven tutorials.
- **Infrastructure**: Transitioning all monitoring to self-hosted/free alternatives.
