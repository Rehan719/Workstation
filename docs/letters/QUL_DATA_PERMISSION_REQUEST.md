Assalamu alaikum,

I am writing for Workstation, a learning platform that is not yet open to the public. Its Quran tools help a person
memorise and revise. They show the text of a verse as fetched from a named source, hide its words for recall practice,
and set side by side verses that share wording. The Arabic is never composed or changed, recitation is never scored,
and no learner is ranked. The Quran tools are not sold and are not part of any paid tier.

We would like to use these parts of the library's data, if you permit it:

1. the mushaf page layouts, to set out a portion by page and line as the printed copy does;
2. the similar-verse lists, beside the lists we compute ourselves by exact shared wording;
3. the word timings for recitations, to light each word as a reciter's audio plays.

We read the library's pages on 12 October 2026 and again on 22 November 2026. Its code is under the MIT licence,
which covers the software and its documentation, and we do not read it as covering the data. Your FAQ says that
rights vary by resource, and the licence tab of each of the 144 resources we checked says that you have no
copyright information for it. So we are asking before we use any of it.

If you permit it, please tell us:

- where each of the resources above came from, and who holds its rights, since they may sit with a Mushaf's
  publisher or a reciter;
- which of the data above we may use, and on what conditions, including the wording of any credit and any link;
- whether we may keep a copy on our own server, or should fetch the data each time it is used;
- whether anything else applies to a platform that also offers paid services unrelated to the Quran tools.

We will credit the library as you ask, change none of the data, and stop using it if you withdraw permission.
