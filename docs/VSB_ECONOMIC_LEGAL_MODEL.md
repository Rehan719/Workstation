## 1. Concept — what the VSB *is*, economically

Each generated VSB is a **living, autonomous economic entity** that earns, self-manages, and distributes value under governance — a **hybrid** of three forms:

- **Waqf (Islamic endowment):** a perpetual, irrevocable dedication of assets whose *usufruct* (income) flows to defined beneficiaries and charitable causes in perpetuity. Embeds the Owner's halal/beneficence values structurally.
- **Trust:** assets held and managed by a trustee (here, the AI governance under the Board/Chief) for beneficiaries (Owner, users, causes) under a deed of terms.
- **Global Multinational (profit-making):** a scalable commercial enterprise that earns revenue, can operate across jurisdictions, and reinvests for growth.

The VSB **autonomously and intelligently self-manages** its legally-compliant **accountancy, finance, and operations** on behalf of the Owner — under the Chief (your digital twin) → Board → AI CEO → CFO, with the **gaas.v5 constitutional gate** and **Change Control** governing material financial actions, and the **DCS append-only audit** preserving every entry.

## 2. Legal Entity Type — user selection at VSB generation

When a user generates their VSB (via Genesis `/establish` or the VSB spawn), they select its legal form. The chosen form configures governance, the distribution waterfall, and compliance templates.

| Option | Nature | Profit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sole Holder** | Individual-owned | To owner | Simplest; owner-centric distribution |
| **Ltd** (Private Limited) | Company, limited liability | Retained/distributed | Default commercial form |
| **PLC** (Public Limited) | Public company | Shareholder distributions | For scale/raising capital |
| **Trust** | Trustee-managed for beneficiaries | To beneficiaries | Deed-defined distribution |
| **Waqf** | Perpetual endowment | Usufruct to causes | Capital preserved; income to beneficiaries/charity |
| **Multinational** | Cross-jurisdiction group | Group distribution | Multi-entity, multi-jurisdiction |
| **Non-profit** | Mission-first, no profit distribution | Reinvested/donated | Surplus → mission |
| **Charity** | Registered charitable purpose | All to cause | Maximal charitable allocation |
| **Hybrid** | Any legally-permissible combination | Configurable | e.g. *Waqf-Ltd*: commercial earnings, endowment governance, charitable waterfall — **the recommended default for the Owner's vision** |

Each option ships as a **template** (governance + compliance + distribution defaults). The app presents these as selectable, with the **Waqf-Ltd Hybrid** as the suggested default.

## 3. Autonomous, Compliant Self-Management

- **Accountancy:** double-entry virtual ledger per VSB (extends the Sovereign Capital Fund / WST unit); chart of accounts; period close; statements (P&L, balance, cash-flow) generated by the CFO agent.
- **Compliance:** jurisdiction-aware rule pack (taxes, filing, charity/Waqf rules) — *configured to the ruled jurisdiction — UK/London (Owner decision 2026-06-21, UNDERSTANDING §9.3)*; every distribution passes the gaas.v5 gate; material actions — a distribution cycle whose estimated distributable profit, or an inter-VSB transfer whose amount, is at or above `ECONOMY_MATERIALITY_WST` (default 250,000 WST) — are held for the Owner's decision (filed as a CRITICAL-tier `economy_material` Change Control request — the Owner's ruling of 2026-09-14, W464: only the Owner's explicit decision releases or refuses one).
- **Operations:** revenue recognition from delivered products/services; cost tracking; reserve management — all autonomous, all audited (DCS append-only).
- **Reporting:** a live financial **Board Pack** assembled on demand for the Owner; adherence to the ≤5-minute staleness invariant.

## 4. Profit-Distribution Waterfall (the heart of the model)

Net profit (after legally-required reserves) flows through an ordered, configurable waterfall. **Defaults below — approved by the Owner 2026-06-21 (UNDERSTANDING §9.1); adjustable per VSB within template bounds (W215).** Percentages are of *distributable profit* after Stage 0.

| # | Stage | Default | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Legal & operating reserves** | off-the-top | Tax, compliance, operating costs, prudential reserve — legally first |
| **1** | **Owner payment** | **20%** | Adjustable payment to you (the repo owner) — your share of success |
| **2** | **Self-investment** | **30%** | Growth, resourcing, continual development of capabilities/utility/productivity (for self, users, developers, owner) |
| **3** | **Capital Investment Fund** | **20%** | Into the VSB's own Sovereign Capital Fund (compounding endowment capital) |
| **4** | **User-project investment** | **15%** | Competitively-selected user projects with high outcome/value/benefit potential |
| **5** | **Charitable giving (Sadaqah/Waqf)** | **15%** | Intelligently-selected causes by real-world urgency/gravity for maximal relief |

All proportions are **adjustable by the Owner** (via the Chief/Board) and **bounded by the entity template** (e.g. a Charity form forces Stage 5 high; a Waqf preserves capital so Stage 3 is endowment). Every cycle's split is logged to the UEG.

## 5. Charitable-Giving Intelligence (Stage 5)

Donations are **intelligently allocated**, not arbitrary:
- **Live signal ingestion:** reputable humanitarian/disaster/needs data feeds *(ruled 2026-06-21 — UNDERSTANDING §9.5; the live-signals seam is Owner-gated)*.
- **Categorisation & scoring:** each candidate cause scored on **urgency × gravity × reach × marginal-impact-of-funds × trust/effectiveness**, then ranked.
- **Allocation:** the charitable budget is distributed to top-ranked causes for **maximal outcome impact, value, and relief**, with diversification and anti-fraud checks.
- **Ethics:** halal-compliant causes; Owner-set inclusions/exclusions honoured; full transparency (every grant auditable).
- **Governance:** allocations pass the gaas.v5 gate; large grants route to Change Control; the Board's Director of Finance + Director of Governance oversee.

## 6. User-Project Investment (Stage 4)
- Users' projects/VSBs are **competitively selected** on potential **outcome success, value, and benefit** (an AI scoring + ranking analogous to the charity engine, but for ventures).
- Investments are tracked as portfolio positions in the Capital Fund; returns recycle into the waterfall — a virtuous, compounding ecosystem.

## 7. Owner Payments (Stage 1)
- An **adjustable** proportion of profit accrues to you, on a schedule you set (e.g. monthly), as **virtual ledger credits** by default.
- **Real-money payout** (bank/processor) is **out of scope until you explicitly authorise it** and a compliance/KYC review is passed (ruled 2026-06-21 — UNDERSTANDING §9.2, §9.6: virtual WST now, Stripe later). The design keeps a clean seam so real rails can be added later behind that gate.

## 8. Governance & Controls
- **Hierarchy:** Owner → Chief (your digital twin) → Board (Director of Finance, Director of Governance) → AI CEO → CFO.
- **gaas.v5 gate** on every distribution; **Change Control** for material/critical financial actions; **DCS append-only** ledger; **arms-length agency** (operational agents cannot rewrite the distribution policy — only the Board, on your direction, can).
- **Materiality approvals (as built, W463 — `agentic_core/economy/governance.py`).** A distribution cycle whose estimated distributable (declared or recognised revenue plus pending venture returns and inter-VSB receipts, less costs and reserves at the reserve rate), or an inter-VSB transfer (a contract settlement included) whose amount, is at or above `ECONOMY_MATERIALITY_WST` (default 250,000 WST — virtual only) does not run: the economy files a Change Control hold (CRITICAL tier — the Owner's ruling of 2026-09-14, W464; it fell to MEDIUM by the tier map's default until then) identified by what filed it (`change_type` `economy_material`, `submitted_by` `economy:<api|heartbeat|transfer>`, the VSB, and for a transfer its counterparty). `POST /api/v1/cca/submit` refuses that type and the hold titles (422). Below the threshold the action proceeds without a hold; a gate error on a material amount holds.
  - **Who decides (W464, FU-014).** Only the Owner's explicit decision: `override_decision` with `admin_decision_for_critical: true` (with auth on, an admin principal) — the Governance hub's Sovereign Sanctum sends exactly that. A review records a recommendation and holds it (`hold_reason` `critical_requires_admin_decision`); no §17.5 pre-validation is run for an economy hold (its implement path never reads one). A record is decided under its effective tier, so a hold filed as MEDIUM before the ruling is decided as CRITICAL too. The gate releases only an approval recorded as the Owner's explicit decision (`decision_source` `admin_override`): an approval a review made before the ruling — or one with no decision recorded — releases nothing; the next request withdraws it, saying why, and asks again as a fresh CRITICAL hold, and `POST /api/v1/cca/{id}/implement` can retire it. Rejections a review made before the ruling still stand (refusing is the restrictive side) and say what made them.
  - **One live record per action.** The gate's decisions for one (VSB, action kind, counterparty), and any give-back, run under one per-action lock. While a hold is `submitted` its amount is re-estimated as the intake changes; once `under_review` it is not, and the review is refused (409) when the `expected_est_distributable_wst` it sends differs from the hold, or when the amount moved during the review.
  - **What an approval releases.** One action, spent once (the record becomes `implemented` when spent): a transfer of at most the approved amount to the same counterparty; an API cycle on at most the revenue it declared, at least the costs it declared and at most the returns/receipts it was filed with; a heartbeat cycle on exactly the recognised events it was filed for that are still pending. A cycle's release is re-estimated at the request's reserve rate; no release exceeds the approved amount, and none is a release of nothing. Intake that arrives later waits for the next cycle. `POST /api/v1/cca/{id}/implement` refuses an approved economy hold the gate can still release (409) and retires one that can never be released as `withdrawn` (admin only when auth is enabled).
  - **Give-back.** When the spent action never ran (the gaas.v5 gate blocked it before it started, or a failed transfer's sender ledger shows no debit), exactly that spend is restored to `approved`, under the same lock — and not when a newer record for the action has been approved, spent, rejected, taken under review, or is a hold that approval could not release. A spend counts as the approved action having run only once the action writes a `released_action_ran` marker on the record (a cycle as it starts; a transfer once its debit has posted). A cycle that raised after it started, or a transfer whose debit posted or cannot be ruled out, keeps the approval spent and says so in the UEG.
  - **Rejection.** A rejection refuses exactly the action it was filed for (status `rejected_by_change_control`, carrying `rejected_by`) while it is the newest decided record for that action (a spend included). While no approved action is known to have run since the rejection, a changed action — or the same action while a spend is unmarked — is filed as a new hold with `follows_rejection`, so the Owner sees the earlier refusal. (Before W464 that link was what kept a review from deciding such a hold; every hold is CRITICAL now, so it is information.) The Governance hub's Sanctum lists every material hold with the other CRITICAL changes under "Awaiting an explicit decision", with the amount. Every Change Control decision on a hold made since W464 — the Owner's approval or rejection, and a retirement through `/implement` — is also written to the UEG (`cca.change_approved` / `cca.change_rejected` / `cca.change_retired`, FU-013); the gate's own withdrawals (superseded, unreleasable, or an approval not the Owner's) reach it as `economy.materiality_hold_withdrawn`.
- **Transparency:** live financial Board Pack + UEG audit trail; you can inspect or adjust any proportion at any time.
