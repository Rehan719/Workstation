## Innovate UK

- Innovate UK describes itself as the UK's innovation agency, backing deep tech businesses in the UK's priority sectors with funding, expert support and connections that help them start, scale and stay in the UK. [observed: UKRI, Innovate UK]
- Its funding opportunities are listed on its own pages, each with its own eligibility, opening and closing dates; none listed on 2026-11-20 was read as one this business could apply for. [observed: UKRI, Innovate UK]
- Velocity, launching in autumn 2027, is to be Innovate UK's new model of support, through which all its support will be offered: one application, for businesses in six Industrial Strategy priority areas (digital and technologies, which includes artificial intelligence, among them) with genuine potential to grow and signs that the potential is being realised. [observed: UKRI, Velocity: long-term support for innovative businesses]
- Velocity is not open now, and nothing read says this business would be accepted into it; it is a route to watch, not one to rely on. [inference]
